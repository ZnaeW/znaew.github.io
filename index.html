Background

I'm a Biotechnology Engineer who found his way into software through the lab. It started with Python scripts to avoid repeating manual analysis — and turned into building complete systems. My path has taken me from genomic data pipelines to full-stack web applications.

Today I work as a backend and full-stack developer, building APIs, data processing services, and the infrastructure that connects them. My background in biotech gives me a different lens: I think about problems from the domain first, then choose the right tools — not the other way around.

I've published research in bioinformatics and comparative genomics, including first-author work on bacterial genome assembly. I continue to believe that the best engineering comes from deeply understanding the problem, not just the framework.

First Author
Genome sequencing and analysis of the first complete genome of Lactobacillus kunkeei strain MP2, an Apis mellifera gut isolate ↗
Sequenced and assembled the first complete genome of L. kunkeei MP2 (1.6M nt) from Chilean honeybee gut microbiota. Built a full bioinformatics pipeline from raw PacBio reads to functional annotation. Pangenome analysis across 17 strains identified 113 unique genes linked to phage insertions and environmental adaptation.

Genome Sequence of Vibrio VPAP30, Isolated from an Episode of Massive Mortality of Reared Larvae of the Scallop Argopecten purpuratus ↗
Draft genome assembly (5.2 Mb, 63 contigs) of the pathogenic Vibrio strain responsible for a complete larval collapse in a Chilean scallop hatchery. The first systematic worldwide catalog of urban microbial ecosystems.
