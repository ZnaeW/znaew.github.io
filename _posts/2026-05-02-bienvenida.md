This is the first entry of a small space I'm opening here, alongside the rest of the portfolio. Nothing fancy, just a place to write down ideas, decisions, and lessons that don't fit naturally inside a project description.

I work somewhere between biology and software. Most days that means writing backend code, designing data models, or shipping web applications. Some days it still means thinking about genomes, sequencing pipelines, and the kind of problems that started my career years ago. The blog is meant to live in that overlap.

## What to expect

Short, focused notes. Mostly about engineering decisions, sometimes about bioinformatics, occasionally about the odd intersections between the two. I'd rather publish a tight 400 word entry than a sprawling 4000 word one.

## Why a blog at all

Two reasons. The first is selfish: writing forces me to clarify what I think. If I can't explain a decision in plain words, I probably haven't fully understood it yet. The second is that I find tremendous value in other people's notes, the small ones especially, and I want to contribute back to that.

If something here is useful to you, that's a great outcome. If it isn't, that's fine too. Either way, thanks for stopping by.
